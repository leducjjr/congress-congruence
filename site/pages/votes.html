How the type and result filters work

They're read from the official wording of each vote. Procedural covers cloture, motions to table, proceed, recommit, adjourn, reconsider or waive, the previous question, quorum calls and appeals of the chair's ruling. Passage covers final passage, motions to suspend the rules and pass, concurring in the other chamber's changes, conference reports, resolutions, veto overrides and treaty ratifications. Amendments are votes on amendments themselves; a motion to table an amendment counts as procedural. Topic is the policy area Congress.gov assigns to the bill voted on, so votes not tied to a bill (most nominations, for example) have none.
