See how your members of Congress vote, and how it compares with the people they represent.

Every recorded House and Senate vote, set beside what independent surveys found people in each state and district want. No party talking points, no grades: the record, and the sources to check it.

Your members

Every vote, compared with what people back home said

Pick your state and a member. You'll see their recent votes, attendance, and, on bills that large independent surveys asked about, whether their vote matched what most people they represent wanted.

  • Senators are compared with their state, representatives with their district
  • Too close to call? It's shown, and never counted either way
  • Each survey source is shown on its own
See an example →

Hide party labels

Judge the record, not the label

One switch at the top of every page removes party names, party vote splits and party figures everywhere on the site. What's left is what the member actually did.

  • Works on every page, and your browser remembers it
  • Share a link that opens with labels hidden: add ?parties=hidden
Try it →

Compare

Put any two members side by side

How often each one missed votes, how often they voted the same way when both voted, every vote where they split, and how each compared with the people they represent.

  • Current or former members, either chamber
  • Every split vote opens to the full roll call

States

Your whole delegation on one page

Each state's senators and representatives, today and in the past, with how their surveyed votes compared with the people they represent, and what the state's residents told each survey.

  • Listed by seat, never ranked
  • Statewide survey results, with how the senators voted on each
See Texas →

Surveyed votes

What people told surveys, and how every member voted

For each survey question matched to a vote: support in every state or district, the range it likely falls in, and each member's vote beside it.

  • Surveys from 2008 to 2025, plus state ballot measures
  • Only votes on the same proposal are counted; related votes are labeled
Browse surveyed votes →

All votes

Every floor vote, searchable and filterable

Filter by chamber, Congress, type of vote, result, topic, or only the votes a survey asked about. Open any vote to see how each member voted.

  • House and Senate votes from 1989, each one checked against the published totals
  • Links to the official record and the bill on Congress.gov
