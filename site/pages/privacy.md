# Privacy

<p class="lede">This site doesn't collect personal information. There are no accounts, no cookies and no
advertising or tracking scripts.</p>

Questions about privacy: <span data-email="privacy">privacy (at) {{email_domain}}</span>.

## What we don't do {#dont}

- We don't ask for your name, email or any account.
- We don't set cookies or use analytics that identify visitors.
- We don't sell, share or store information about what you look at.
- If address lookup is added, the address will be used once to find your members and never stored.

## What stays in your browser {#browser}

So that search results can show how *your* members voted, the site remembers the last three members
you looked at, in your browser's own storage on your device. It is never sent to us and isn't a cookie.
The **forget** link on the Search page clears it, as does clearing your browser's site data.

## What any web server records {#server-logs}

Like almost every website, our web server keeps a standard log of requests (the address of the device
asking, the page requested, and the time) to keep the site secure and working. These logs are deleted
automatically after about two weeks and aren't used for anything else.

## Things your browser fetches from elsewhere {#third-parties}

- **Typeface:** pages load the Public Sans typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google
  to download it.
- **Links** to official records, Congress.gov and survey organizations take you to those sites, which
  have their own privacy policies.
