# Install the app

<p class="lede">You can add this site to your phone's home screen or your computer so it opens like an app:
full screen, with its own icon. It's free and takes a few seconds. There's no app store, no account and
no tracking.</p>

## What you get {#what}

- **Its own icon** on your home screen, dock or Start menu, opening without the browser's address bar.
- **Pages you've already viewed work without a signal**, such as on a plane or in a building with no
  reception. Pages you haven't opened yet need a connection.
- **Always current.** It's the same site, so every update reaches the app straight away. There's nothing
  to update by hand.
- **Shortcuts:** press and hold the icon (or right-click it on a computer) to jump straight to Find your
  members, Surveyed votes or Compare.
- **A Back button** in the header, since there's no browser bar. Swiping back works too.

## iPhone and iPad {#iphone}

1. Open [uscongressvsvoters.org](https://uscongressvsvoters.org) in **Safari**, **Chrome** or **Edge**
   (Chrome and Edge need iOS 16.4 or later).
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up). In Safari it's at the bottom of the
   screen on iPhone and at the top on iPad; in Chrome it's at the right end of the address bar.
3. Scroll **down** the list of actions (below Add to Bookmarks, Find in Page and Print) and tap
   **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**.

Don't see **Add to Home Screen**? Scroll to the very bottom of the list, tap **Edit Actions…**, and tap the
green **+** next to **Add to Home Screen**. It then stays in your share list.

## Android {#android}

1. Open [uscongressvsvoters.org](https://uscongressvsvoters.org) in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu at the top right.
3. Tap **Add to Home screen**, then **Install**. (Chrome sometimes offers **Install app** at the bottom of
   the screen on its own; that does the same thing.)

In **Samsung Internet**, tap the menu (☰), then **Add page to**, then **Home screen**. In **Firefox**, tap
the menu (⋮), then **Add to Home screen**.

## Computer {#computer}

- **Chrome:** click the install icon at the right end of the address bar (a small screen with a down
  arrow), then **Install**. Or open the **⋮** menu, then **Cast, save and share**, then
  **Install page as app**.
- **Microsoft Edge:** open the **…** menu, then **Apps**, then **Install this site as an app**.
- **Safari on a Mac** (macOS Sonoma or later): open the **File** menu, then **Add to Dock**.
- **Firefox** on a computer doesn't install sites as apps. A bookmark works just as well.

Menu names change from time to time. If yours look different, look for "Install" or "Add to Home Screen"
in the browser's menu.

## Privacy {#privacy}

The app is this website, so it collects exactly what the website does: nothing. There are no accounts,
no cookies and no tracking, and it doesn't contact any other company. To work without a signal, your
browser keeps copies of pages you've viewed on your device only; they're replaced with fresh ones
whenever you're online. See [Privacy](privacy.html#browser).

## Removing it {#remove}

Remove it like any app: press and hold the icon and choose **Remove App** or **Uninstall**, or on a
computer, right-click it (or use the app's menu) and choose **Uninstall**. Clearing this site's data in
your browser removes the saved copies too.
