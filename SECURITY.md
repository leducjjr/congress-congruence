# Security

To report a security problem with the site or this code, email
**security (at) uscongressvsvoters.org** (write the address out in full). Please don't open a public
issue for it. We'll acknowledge your report and keep you informed of the fix.

The site's contact details for this are also published at
https://uscongressvsvoters.org/.well-known/security.txt.

The site stores no information about visitors: no accounts, no cookies, no analytics. Messages sent
through its contact form are emailed to the project's inbox and not stored on the server.
